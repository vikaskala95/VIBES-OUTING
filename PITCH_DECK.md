## 📋 Slide 2: The Problem

### 🔴 What's broken today?

| Pain Point | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Loneliness Epidemic** | 64% of young Indians want to explore but have no group to go with |
| **No Platform** | No single app to discover, join, and pay for group outings |
| **Trust Gap** | Going with strangers feels unsafe — no verification, no reviews |
| **Coordination Chaos** | Planning group outings via WhatsApp is messy — payments, headcounts, logistics |
| **High Cost** | Solo travel is expensive; group sharing reduces cost by 40-60% |

> *"I want to go trekking this weekend, but none of my friends are interested."* — Every 20-something ever.

---

## 📋 Slide 3: The Solution

### 🟢 Furzi Outing — One-stop Group Outing Platform

```
📱 Browse → 💳 Book → 👥 Meet → 🎉 Explore!
```

**Core Value:**
- **Curated outings** by verified organizers
- **Token-based booking** (₹50-₹2500) via Razorpay
- **Solo-friendly** — join as individual, get matched with a group
- **Group-friendly** — book for your squad in one click
- **Safe** — ID verification, emergency contacts, organizer ratings
- **Social** — in-app group chat, reviews, interest matching
